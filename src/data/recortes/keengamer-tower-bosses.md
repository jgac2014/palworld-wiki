### Zoe

> Zoe and Grizzbolt wait inside the Tower of the Rayne Syndicate , a short trip from the starting area in the early grasslands. The duo fights at Level 10 as a pure Electric boss, so Ground Pals counter everything Grizzbolt throws at you.

### Lily

> Lily and Lyleen defend the Tower of the Free Pal Alliance , just past the red hills north of the first tower. They fight at Level 20 as a pure Grass boss, and Fire damage tears through Lyleen’s health bar.

### Axel

> Axel and Orserk sit at the summit of the volcano in the southwest, inside the Tower of the Brothers of the Eternal Pyre . The pair fights at Level 30 , and Orserk’s dual Electric/Dragon typing gives you two weaknesses to exploit at once.

### Marcus

> Marcus rides Faleris in the far northeast corner of the desert, inside the Tower of the PIDF . The duo fights at Level 40 as a pure Fire boss, and Water Pals douse Faleris’s flames with ease.

### Victor

> Save carryover : On existing saves, tower boss missions are automatically marked as completed based on your past victories.
